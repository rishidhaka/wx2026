How the Bracket Works
In Phase 1 you pick your group standings first.
Your predicted bracket is then auto-seeded based on those picks — 1st place in Group A vs 2nd place in Group B, etc.
You then pick winners through to the Final.

Rolling updates: Points are awarded as each match finishes — no waiting until the end.
