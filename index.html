How the Bracket Works
In Phase 1 you pick your group standings first.
Your predicted bracket is then auto-seeded based on those picks — 1st place in Group A vs 2nd place in Group B, etc.
You then pick winners through to the Final.

Rolling updates: Points are awarded as each match finishes — no waiting until the end.

📅 Phase 1: Pre-Tournament Predictions

Deadline: June 17, 2026 at 11:59 PM (after first round of group matches)

⚠️ ONE-TIME SUBMISSION: Once you save your picks, they lock permanently. You cannot edit them after submission to preserve competitive integrity.

  • Group Standings: Drag teams to predict the full order in each of 12 groups (1pt for each exact position, including 4th)
  • Third-Place Qualifiers: Pick which 8 of 12 third-placed teams advance to Round of 32 (2pts each = 16pts max)
  • Knockout Bracket: Predict R32 → R16 → Quarters → Semis → Final (auto-seeded from your group picks)
  • Golden Boot: Pick the tournament's top scorer (5pts)

⚡ Phase 2: Second Chance

Starts: June 27, 2026 (after group stage ends)

  • Real Bracket: Predict R16 → Final using the actual qualified teams (5pts per R16/QF, 10pts per SF, 15pts for Champion)
  • Fresh Start: Bad Phase 1 picks? Phase 2 keeps you competitive!
  • Updated Golden Boot: Change your pick if needed (5pts)
